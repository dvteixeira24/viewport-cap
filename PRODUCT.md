# viewport-cap

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Native Windows desktop (WPF/.NET 8); the design tooling's web/iOS/Android platform enumeration does not cover this application.

## Product Purpose

A tray-first app that records a selected screen region, then previews, trims, and exports MP4, WebM, or GIF.

## Users

People recording a region of their Windows desktop. More specific audience positioning is not established.

## Operating Context

Windows 10 version 2004 or newer and Windows 11, x64. Start from the tray or global hotkey, select a region, confirm the countdown, stop from the HUD or hotkey, then review and export.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve capture, audio selection, trimming, export options, keyboard shortcuts, saved settings, confirmation dialogs, and temporary-recording cleanup. FFmpeg is required for GIF/WebM and MP4 trimming. No new capture or export features are requested.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the viewport-cap name. The user confirmed a consistent refresh of all app windows and a familiar Windows appearance with accessible controls, comfortable spacing, and clear hierarchy.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md and the native implementation in src/ViewportCap.App. There are no confirmed commercial claims or marketing assets to add.
